Department Overview

Vision, Mission & Statutory Duties

The Legal Department advises the Government of Gujarat on all legal, statutory, and constitutional matters, coordinates litigation on behalf of the State, and oversees the administration of justice.

1. Legislative Drafting

Scrutinizing and drafting all government bills, ordinances, and statutory rules introduced in the Gujarat Legislative Assembly.

2. Litigation Defense

Defending public interest in matters pending before the Supreme Court of India, High Court of Gujarat, and Subordinate Courts through designated Law Officers.

3. Legal Aid & Lok Adalats

Ensuring no citizen is denied justice by reason of economic disability through statutory legal aid clinics, mediation, and Lok Adalat benches.
